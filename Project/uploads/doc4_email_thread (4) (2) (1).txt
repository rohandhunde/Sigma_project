Team,

Responses to your questions:

1. Tolerance-based matching: Yes, confirmed. Our reconciliation engine supports
   configurable tolerance thresholds. The 0.5% variance you mentioned is well within
   our standard configuration range.

2. Annual licence — IMPORTANT UPDATE: The USD 48,000 figure in our earlier proposal
   was quoted per-entity. For four entities as you describe, the correct annual licence
   would be USD 162,000/year. We apologise for the ambiguity in the original proposal.
   We can offer a group discount bringing this to USD 144,000/year if all four entities
   are contracted simultaneously.

3. Data residency: We deploy on Microsoft Azure. Azure has a UAE North data centre
   (Abu Dhabi). All data can be configured to remain within this region. We can include
   a data residency addendum in the contract.

4. Warranty: Standard 90-day warranty post go-live, covering defect resolution at no
   additional cost. Extended warranty available at 8% of annual licence per year.

Please note the revised total cost of ownership for year one: USD 195,000 (implementation)
+ USD 144,000 (group licence, year 1) = USD 339,000.

Kind regards,
Vikram
